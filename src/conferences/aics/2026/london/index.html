Don't Miss the AI Revolution in Software Development

Discover how AI is revolutionizing software engineering at the AI Coding Summit — a hybrid event streaming online worldwide on July 6–7, with a live in-person gathering in London on July 6th from 2pm to 6pm. Dive into cutting-edge talks and hands-on workshops on AI-powered software development.

Network with industry leaders, explore game-changing tools, and stay ahead in the fast-evolving world of AI-driven development. Join online from anywhere or attend the London gathering, and do not miss the biggest shift in software engineering.
