FAQ

Organisational info

  • You will get an email with all the information you’ll need at the conference about 2-3 days before the event & we'll duplicate the message on the day of the conference before the stream.

    If you didn't receive the email, please check your spam folder.

    And if you still haven't found the email, please contact us via events@gitnation.org.

Workshops

  • Free Workshops are the complementary part of Full Tickets (Remote Full ticket and Hybrid Ticket). They will be run remotely via Zoom. The registration will be available via gitnation.com when we finalize the schedule of the workshops. You’ll get an email about that and the instructions on how to register 1 month before the first workshop.

    As per Pro Workshops, they are not included in Full tickets and should be bought separately. Pay attention that some Pro Workshops might be held in-person, and some – remotely via Zoom.

Tickets

  • Yes, it is possible to pay via invoice. Please, send your company details here and we will issue it for you:

    • company name
    • company address
    • VAT number
    • number and type of ticket(s) you're interested in

    As soon as we receive the payment, we'll send you a code to activate the ticket(s).

  • Yes, you can. Simply log in to your ti.to account and re-assign the ticket to this person. Pay attention that only a ticket buyer can re-assign tickets.

  • You can easily change the name in the ticket in your ti.to account. Just log in and edit the attendee information. Pay attention that only the ticket buyer can do that.

  • You can participate remotely via GitNation Multipass.

    Multipass holders enjoy a 15% discount across all our in-person events. Applicable to a single non-combo ticket or a PRO workshop per event.

    Subscribers with one renewal in the past year also get a 15% COMBO ticket discounts. If a subscriber has taken a pause in their membership, they will need to wait for one more year and complete another renewal to receive this type of the discount.

    To participate in-person you can contact us here to upgrade your ticket and get a 15% discount to attend the event.

    Please note that the discount does not apply to Early Bird tickets either.

    To learn more about Multipass please visit this page.

Recordings

  • If you are a Multipass or Full ticket holder you'll get early access to talk recordings at gitnation.com.

    Note, the publication of the recordings from hybrid day can take around 1-2 weeks as they require more time for post-production. While the recordings from remote days are usually available the next day.

  • The recordings will be published on gitnation.com and will be shared with Full ticket and Multipass holders within 3-4 weeks.

Live Stream

  • The conference will be streamed on a separate watch page. You’ll get the instructions on how to access it via email the day before and one more email on the day of the conference.

    Alternatively, you can access the watch page from the main conference website. Check the website on the conference day – there will be button 'watch livestream'. Click it and follow the instructions.

Technical questions (Remote attendance)

  • Please check under which email you're trying to open the stream (should be the same email under which you have a conference ticket / Multipass / badge registration).

    If it doesn't work, please contact us here.

Other

  • Yes, of course! We’d be happy if you become our info-partner. Please, drop us a line at events@gitnation.org and we’ll get back to you with all the information.

Refunds

  • No, we do not provide refunds in this case due to our policies. Please check our T&C here.
