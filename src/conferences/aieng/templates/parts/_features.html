# 80 speakers · 3 tracks [ photo: main stage, wide ]

80 engineers and tech leaders sharing insights

The staff engineers shipping with agents every day, the CTOs and VPs who have rebuilt their organizations around them, and the people building the tools and the open source in between. Two days with the people who already made the expensive mistakes, so your team doesn't have to.

# Production-tested [ photo: workshop room ]

In-depth, hands-on workshops

Build the thing: agent harnesses, eval suites, context pipelines, adoption playbooks. Led by the people who run them at work. Leave with a setup that works, not a list of things to try.

# Lunch · both days [ photo: food trucks ]

The Food Truck Fest

A line-up of food trucks for lunch, plus good coffee all day, fresh fruit, salads and other healthy options, pastries and sweet treats to keep you powered.

# Evening [ photo: party, wide ]

The biggest AI engineering party in Europe

Live artists, shows and activities for 1,500 engineers and tech leaders, run by the crew behind Europe's biggest developer parties.

Amsterdam exploration day

Explore Amsterdam with new friends during our boat and walking tours. Canals, the museum quarter, the markets, and the conversations that only happen once you are off the conference floor.

Networking that works

Every talk continues in a speaker Q&A room. Discussion tables are grouped by adoption stage, so you sit with teams at your stage who already solved the problems you are facing now.

# from Central Station · 5 min [ photo: branded ferry crossing the IJ ]

Arrive by the AI Ferry

The venue sits straight across the IJ from Amsterdam Central. Step off the train, onto the AI Engineering ferry, and cross the water with the people you are about to spend two days with.

The venue opens straight onto the water. Between sessions the riverside turns into a festival: terraces, live music, long tables and 1,500 people comparing notes in the May sun.
